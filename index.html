The Sims 4 Mod Launcher

By DrBerg

A Windows app for one player on their own PC. It searches CurseForge for The Sims 4 mods, shows the mod information, and downloads the file from CurseForge into that player's own Mods folder.

Turning a mod off only moves the file into a local Disabled folder next to Mods. The app does not upload, rehost, or share mods.
